# Part 3: Engineering Response & Automation
SRE is not just operations; it is software engineering applied to infrastructure problems. This section details how to scale systems through automation, eliminate operational toil, and shift from reactive firefighting to proactive engineering.

## Core Topics Covered
* **Automation Philosophy:** Determining when to automate, when *not* to automate, and mitigating the risks of automated mass destructive actions.
* **Toil Reduction:** Strategies for tracking, profiling, and capping manual operational work to ensure SREs spend at least 50% of their time on engineering.
* **Release Engineering:** Standardizing deployment pipelines, canary deployments, and automated rollbacks to secure the software delivery supply chain.
