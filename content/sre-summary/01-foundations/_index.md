# Part 1: Foundations of Reliability
Before managing complex distributed systems, we have to define what "reliability" actually means to the business and the user. This section establishes the fundamental metrics and cultural shifting required to run a successful SRE practice.

## Core Topics Covered
As you browse through this chapter, we will unpack:
* **SLIs, SLOs, and SLAs:** Defining quantitative metrics that matter.
* **Error Budgets:** How to turn mathematical reliability targets into a business tool that balances velocity and stability.
* **Toil Management:** Identifying and ruthlessly eliminating repetitive, manual operational work through engineering.
