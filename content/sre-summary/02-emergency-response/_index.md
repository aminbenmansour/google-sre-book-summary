# Part 2: Emergency Response & Incident Management
When systems fail, engineers don't panic—they execute. This section focuses on the tactical and operational frameworks required to manage critical outages, minimize Time to Resolution (TTR), and maintain operational sanity under pressure.

## Core Topics Covered
* **Incident Management Architecture:** Dissecting the roles of the Incident Commander, Communications Lead, and Ops Lead to prevent chaotic triage.
* **Blameless Postmortems:** Cultivating a psychological safety culture that fixes the system vulnerabilities rather than punishing human mistakes.
* **On-Call Dynamics:** Balancing page loads, alert fatigue, and defining sustainable operational rotations.
