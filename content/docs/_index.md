# Site Reliability Engineering Summary
This section contains a deep-dive analysis of SRE methodologies, heavily inspired by production standards and Google SRE frameworks. It bridges theoretical reliability concepts with practical engineering execution.

## Chapters
The material is broken down into four core pillars:
1. **Foundations:** Service Level Objectives (SLOs), Error Budgets, and the SRE mindset.
2. **Emergency Response:** Incident management, postmortems, and mitigating outages.
3. **Engineering Response:** Automation, scale management, and reducing toil.
4. **Advanced Systems:** Complex distributed architectures and deep infrastructure patterns.
